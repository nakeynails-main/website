Our Story

Why we
made this.

My wife is a physiotherapist, which means she's at the sink washing her hands dozens of times a day. Over time, that constant exposure really took a toll. Her nails became brittle, dry, and started peeling, even though she took good care of them.

We tried everything people usually recommend. ISDIN, OPI, the so-called heavy hitters. Some were too harsh for her skin. Others just didn't work. Nothing felt made for someone like her.

At some point, I stopped looking for a better product and decided to create one for her myself.

The idea was simple. A gentle, water-based formula. No alcohol. No formaldehyde. Just ingredients that actually support nail repair.

It had to be something she could use between patients, quickly and without disrupting her day. Because the only treatments that work are the ones you can actually stick to.

No complicated routines. No harsh treatments. Just something that works, even on your busiest days.

How long until I see results?

+

Most customers notice softer cuticles within the first week and a reduction in peeling by day 14. With consistent use over 4 weeks, nails typically feel thicker and stronger. Results vary depending on the extent of prior damage.

Is it safe?

+

Dermatologist-tested, pregnancy-safe, vegan, and 8-free — no fragrance, formaldehyde, parabens, sulfates, phthalates, camphor, toluene, or formaldehyde resin. One formula. Nothing unnecessary.

Can I wear polish while using it?

+

You can, but for a full recovery, bare nails for four weeks gives the best results. After that, it works as a daily strengthener — use it under or over polish indefinitely.
